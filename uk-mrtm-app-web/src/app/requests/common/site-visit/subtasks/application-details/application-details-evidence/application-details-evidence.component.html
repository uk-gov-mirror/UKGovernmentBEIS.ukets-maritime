When reviewing your application for a virtual site visit, your regulator will consider:

  • the information provided by the verifier on the risk analysis outcome
  • information on how the virtual site visit will be carried out
  • evidence that measures are taken to reduce the verification risk to an acceptable level
  • any proposal to carry out a physical site visit after the virtual site visit

Your regulator may request further information to decide whether or not to approve the virtual site visit.
